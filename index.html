    Where do you fit in RMP's data plan?

    RMP is building a safe way for schools, programs, employers and partners to share what they know. The goal is simple: help more young people get what they need, from early childhood to a good job. Pick who you are to see your part.

    For everyone

    The whole plan at a glance

    Every role above is one part of the same plan. Here is the big picture: why we are doing this, what we are building, who does what and when.

    71,142by 2030

    Why: RMP's goal is for 71,142 more young people to meet cradle-to-career milestones and be on the path to a good income by 2030. To get there, partners need to see who is on track, what works and where young people fall through the cracks. Today, every group holds its own piece of the story. This plan connects the pieces safely. The point is not more data. It is better choices.

    What we are building

    Five connected pieces
    1. RMP's data warehouse

    RMP's own data and reports in one reliable place. RMP owns it.

    2. Shared data ecosystem

    A secure place where partners' records are matched and names are removed. Big Bet #1 goes first.

    3. Tools to improve

    Results Lab helps Target Champions plan, track progress and try new ideas.

    4. Tools people use

    Cities Learn, CCL MARKETPLACE, career tools, a learning hub and a shared resource list.

    5. Statewide links

    A setup other Colorado partnerships can reuse, and a path to state data.

    We are not building one big app for everyone. We are helping the tools people already use work together.
